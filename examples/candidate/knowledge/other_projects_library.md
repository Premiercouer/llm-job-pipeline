### Project 1: Retail Demand Forecasting
**Timeline**: Jan 2026 – Mar 2026
**Project Type**: Course project — time-series forecasting

#### Technical Stack
- Python (pandas, statsmodels, scikit-learn), SQL

#### Key Achievements
- Built weekly SKU-level demand forecasts with SARIMA and gradient boosting
- Back-tested on 52 weeks with rolling-origin cross-validation

#### Quantifiable Results
- MAPE reduced from 18.4% (seasonal naive) to 11.9%

#### Keywords for Job Matching
`Time Series`, `Forecasting`, `SARIMA`, `Gradient Boosting`, `Python`, `Cross-validation`, `Retail Analytics`
